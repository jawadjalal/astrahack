## Thread

1. We built Ignura because launches keep dying in week one. Here is the four-week plan we run for apps. A thread.

2. Week 1: the teardown and the plan. We use the app like a stranger would and write down what we find. It is free.

3. Week 2: polish. App Store screenshots, the landing page, the shots we will need for the film.

4. Week 3: the launch film and the UGC shoot. UGC that opens like a post, not an ad.

5. Week 4: line it up, then launch. X, TikTok and Product Hunt, hour by hour.

6. Want the teardown for your app? It is free. [LINK]
