# AstraHack: 60-second public demo recording

**Entry point:** `https://ignura.com/astrahack?demo=ignura`. This demo mode starts with Ignura's URL prefilled, shows four progress steps over about 10 seconds, then opens a **recorded** Ignura canvas. Keep the **Demo · recorded run** label visible. The ten-second sequence is a presentation of existing results; it is not a new end-to-end computer-use run.

## Record-ready checklist

1. Open the deployed `?demo=ignura` link and verify that the prefilled URL, four progress steps, **Demo · recorded run** label, and recorded canvas actually appear. If deployment is still in progress, use the current preview URL supplied by the operator.
2. Capture the 10-second progress sequence in one take. Do not speed it up or call it a live 10-second fleet run. The underlying Ignura QA run took longer.
3. Pre-frame close-ups of the screenshot trail, actual ad images, and campaign cards. Capture each as a separate take at readable canvas zoom. Show UGC and X only if their cards have landed.
4. Keep **recorded run** legible in the opening or add that label in the edit. The social content is draft copy; nothing is posted to X or Reddit by this flow.
5. Hide notifications, account details, terminals, and keys. Watch the final export once without sound to check each visual claim, then once with sound to check timing.
