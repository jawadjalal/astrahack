## Campaign
**Goal:** Learn which angle, hook and creator pairing earns qualified visits to https://ignura.com/, then put more creators behind the winners.

### Test (Days 1-7)
Find the strongest hook and angle at small cost.
- Publish 5 scripts across tiktok, youtube-shorts, instagram-reels, linkedin, one per day, then repeat each with its alternate hook. Test matrix: hook (A/B per script) x creator x platform. Change one variable at a time, so finish both hooks on a script before changing creator or platform.
- Keep every cut under 35 seconds with the same CTA, so only the hook and angle vary.
- Review 3-second hold rate and link clicks at 24 and 72 hours.
- Pause anything below the benchmark after 72 hours.

### Scale (Days 8-21)
Put more creators behind the winning hook and angle.
- Brief 2 to 3 more creators on the top script.
- Re-cut the winner with two new hooks.
- Cross-post winners to the other platforms.

### Always-on (Days 22-30)
Keep a steady flow of fresh cuts.
- Publish 3 posts a week.
- Refresh hooks weekly from comments and questions.
- Retire a creative when its 3-second hold rate falls.
